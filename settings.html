Promoted content

Posts that appear because a company paid for the spot — not because you follow anyone. LinkedIn labels them "Promoted"; the filter reads that label. Hidden posts leave no notice, so there is nothing to read past.

Already running an ad blocker? Leave these off for now — a known issue where the two can conflict on LinkedIn is being worked on.

Hide promoted posts
A company paid for this spot in your feed. On = these never appear.
Hide boosted partner posts
Someone's real post — "Promoted · Partnership with…" — that a brand paid to amplify. On = hidden.

Labs

Hardcoded patterns that catch phrasing broader than a single typed phrase — verb variants, connector shapes across trigger words. Not editable, unlike Categories. Off by default: expect a different false-positive profile than a plain phrase.

Muted people

Their posts never reach the feed — no notice, no chip, nothing to read past. Mute someone from the ··· menu on any of their posts.

Trusted People

Their posts are never flagged or hidden, no matter what else matches — not a phrase category, not Promoted, not Boosted. Type a name below to add someone.

Activity

Which phrases are doing the work. Counts only — no log of individual posts, because the point of the filter is not reading them.
